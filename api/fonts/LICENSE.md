# Fonts for the share cards

The card renderer needs TrueType files, so these are the TTF builds of the site's own faces:

- **Inter** (400, 800) by Rasmus Andersson, https://github.com/rsms/inter
- **Fraunces** (600) by Undercase Type, https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
