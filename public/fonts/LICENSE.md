# Fonts

Self-hosted latin subsets, served from this site instead of Google Fonts so a slow phone doesn't need two extra connections before the first paint.

- **Geist** by Vercel, the site's typeface; with **Silkscreen** by Jason Kottke it also sets the talk view (the ETHIndia x Devcon template typefaces)
- **Noto Sans Devanagari** by Google (Devanagari subset, loaded only in Hindi; `mohar-deva.woff2` is a 2 KB cut with just the letters of मोहर, for the logo), https://github.com/notofonts/devanagari
